# How to Bake

Follow these instructions precisely in order to bake

## Steps.

1. List the ingredients to combine
  
**Examples**
Combine: flour, water, milk, eggs
Combine: flour, water, butter

2. Bake temp and time

**Examples**
Oven Temp: 350
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Oven Temp: 400
Cook Time: 20 minutes

3. Rest/Cool

**Examples**
Cool Time: 15 minutes
Cool Time: 10 minutes (in pan), then 1 hour (on wire rack)

4. Eat

**Examples**
Eat the cookies
Eat the cake
Eat the muffins

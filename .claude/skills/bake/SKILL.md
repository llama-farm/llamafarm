# How to Bake

Follow these instructions precisely in order to bake

## Steps.

1. List the ingredients to combine
  
**Examples**
Combine: flour, water, milk, eggs
Combine: flour, water, butter

2. Bake temp and time

**Examples**
Oven Temp: 350
Cook Time: 30 minutes

Oven Temp: 400
Cook Time: 20 minutes

3. Rest time

**Examples**
Rest Time: 10 minutes
Rest Time: 15 minutes (cool completely before frosting)

4. Eat

**Examples**
Eat the cookies
Eat the cake
Eat the muffins
